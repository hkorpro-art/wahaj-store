# Wahaj Colors

## Primary

- Deep Crimson: `#450006`

## Supporting

- Soft Ivory: `#FFF7EA`
- Warm Blush Background: `#F3E1E4`
- Deep Text: `#211315`

## Usage

The primary brand impression should come from `#450006` with ivory contrast. Gold should appear only as a small glint or accent, not as the dominant brand system.
