# Wahaj Brand Assets

## Current Assets

- `public/favicon.svg`: primary Brand Icon for browser and search surfaces.
- `public/favicon.ico`: ICO favicon for older browser support.
- `public/icon-192.png`: PWA and Android icon.
- `public/icon-512.png`: high-resolution PWA icon and schema logo fallback.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: iOS home screen icon.
- `public/og-image.png` and `public/og-image.jpg`: Open Graph sharing images.
- `public/twitter-card.png` and `public/twitter-card.jpg`: Twitter/X sharing images.

## Asset Strategy

Use the Brand Icon for small technical surfaces where clarity matters more than full logo detail. Use the full logo only for large UI and campaign placements where the mark can breathe.

## Export Notes

When assets are regenerated, keep the Brand Icon simple: crimson background, ivory symbol, no full WAHAJ wordmark, no accessory descriptor, and no heavy gold detail.
