# Wahaj Brand Guidelines v1.0

## Direction

Wahaj uses a Luxury Minimalism identity: deep crimson, soft ivory, generous spacing, and restrained decorative detail. The brand should feel refined, feminine, and clear without relying on heavy ornamentation.

## Logo Usage

- Use the full Wahaj logo only in larger brand surfaces such as headers, packaging, hero visuals, and campaign designs.
- Use the Brand Icon only for small placements such as browser favicon, Google result icon, app icon, mobile shortcut, and social preview identity marks.
- Do not use the full logo as a favicon because fine details become unclear at 16x16, 32x32, and 48x48.

## Small Icon Rules

- Keep the background deep crimson.
- Keep the symbol ivory or white.
- Do not include the full word WAHAJ.
- Do not include "إكسسوارات نسائية" in icons.
- Avoid dense gold detailing in small assets.

## Color Rule

Gold is not the primary identity color. Use it only as a limited highlight when a design needs a subtle luxury accent.
